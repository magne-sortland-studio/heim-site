Frequently asked

HEIM basics

What is HEIM?

HEIM is a home index for keeping track of the small details that are useful to remember about your home.

What can I save?

You can save household items and useful details such as light bulbs, filters, batteries, paint colors, notes, measurements and other information.

Can I organize things by room?

Yes. Items are organized by home and room so information stays easy to find.

Can I add more than one home?

HEIM is designed to support multiple homes, such as your main home, cabin or another property.

What is the shopping list?

Parts or details marked as needing replacement can be brought together in one shopping list.

Can I add photos?

Yes. You can add photos to make rooms and items easier to recognize.
