Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Overview

HEIM is designed to help you keep track of useful information about your home.

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible and to keep the experience simple and private.

Information you add to HEIM

HEIM may allow you to save information such as home names, room names, household items, notes, replacement details, measurements and photos.

This information is used to provide the app's functionality.

Photos

If you choose to add a photo to an item or room, HEIM may request access to your device's photo library or camera.

HEIM only accesses photos when you choose to use a photo-related feature.

Accounts

HEIM does not require you to create a user account to use the core app.

Data storage

Information you create in HEIM is primarily stored on your device and is used to provide the features of the app.

Removing the app or deleting its local data may remove information stored within HEIM.

Purchases

If HEIM offers optional purchases through the App Store, payment processing is handled by Apple.

HEIM does not receive or store your full payment card information.

Third-party services

HEIM may use third-party services that are necessary to operate certain app features, such as purchase management or app distribution.

Those providers may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

HEIM is a general household utility and is not specifically directed toward children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if HEIM changes or new features are introduced.

The latest version will always be available on this page.
